About Me

I am a seasoned healthcare professional with several years of experience working in the health and medical sector. Having recently moved to the UK from Germany, I have a strong academic background and have undertaken specialist ambulance training and development. Until my recent emigration I held a full time position at Malteser Hilfsdienst e.V. as the German equivalent of an Ambulance Care Assistant. In the UK I took up a role as a Healthcare Assistant with County Durham and Darlington NHS Foundation Trust to build on my previous experience.
More recently however my longheld interest in computers and coding has driven me to reskill as a Full Stack Web Developer. My extensive experience of working in healthcare has made me very adept at thinking out of the box and solving problems, a skill I found exceptionally useful regarding working with code. During my Web Development training I gained considerable experience working with JavaScript as well as the MERN stack, alongside version control, APIs, machine learning, and more. Furthermore I was able to deepen the time-management skills I already learned in my previous and current roles and studies, and apply them to successfully manage both my learning and my current employment.
Thanks to my work and volunteer experience I am familiar with dealing with a wide variety of common and exceptional circumstances and know to take a step back, assess the situation and plot a sensible course of action. In addition to the medical demands of my roles in healthcare (triaging, advanced first aid, ongoing care etc.) I was also able to gain ample experience with communicating clearly and efficiently with people from a wide variety of backgrounds. Moreover I was able to develop advanced user skills with a wide range of professional IT software and hardware. My personal experiences have left me with a deep respect for the merits of diverse and inclusive team work, as well as an ability to step up and take responsibility when appropriate. I am well used to working both in a team and on my own.
Outside of employment I enjoy a range of outdoor activities, and I am a keen amateur writer. Through these I have additionally developed a number of valuable skills in personal and group organisation, time management, and the importance of being prepared for all ventualities. I regularly attend the gym and go hiking, and have recently taken up kayaking. As for my writing I am a frequent contributor to several online communities.
